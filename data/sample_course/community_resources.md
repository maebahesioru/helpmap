# Springfield Community Resource Guide 2026
# A fictional guide for demonstration. All organizations are invented.

## FOOD ASSISTANCE

### Springfield Food Bank
- Address: 1200 Oak Street, Springfield
- Hours: Monday-Friday 9:00-17:00, Saturday 10:00-14:00
- Services: Free groceries, no appointment needed. Bring ID if available; no ID required for first visit.
- Languages: English, Spanish
- Contact: (555) 010-1001

### Riverside Community Kitchen
- Address: 45 River Road, Springfield
- Hours: Daily 11:00-13:00 (hot lunch), Tuesday & Thursday 17:00-19:00 (dinner)
- Services: Free hot meals, no registration, all welcome.
- Languages: English, Spanish, Vietnamese
- Contact: (555) 010-1002

### Weekend Backpack Program
- Address: Springfield Elementary School, 300 School Lane
- Hours: Friday 14:00-17:00 during school terms
- Services: Weekend food packs for families with school-age children. Register through the school office.
- Languages: English, Spanish
- Contact: (555) 010-1003

## HEALTHCARE

### Springfield Community Health Center
- Address: 500 Health Plaza, Springfield
- Hours: Monday-Friday 8:00-18:00, Saturday 9:00-13:00
- Services: Primary care on a sliding fee scale. No insurance required. Walk-ins accepted before 11:00; appointments recommended.
- Languages: English, Spanish, Mandarin
- Contact: (555) 010-2001

### Free Dental Clinic (First Monday)
- Address: 500 Health Plaza, Suite 210
- Hours: First Monday of each month, 9:00-15:00
- Services: Free cleanings and basic dental care for uninsured adults. Arrive early; limited slots.
- Languages: English, Spanish
- Contact: (555) 010-2002

### Mental Health Warmline
- Hours: Daily 8:00-22:00
- Services: Free, confidential emotional support by phone. Not for emergencies — call 911 for emergencies.
- Languages: English, Spanish
- Contact: (555) 010-2003

### Housing Help Desk
